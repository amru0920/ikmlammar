About

Mufti Ismail Menk is a renowned Islamic scholar and motivational speaker from Zimbabwe. He is widely respected for his lectures on peace, tolerance, community building and has spoken across the world inspiring millions.

Mufti Menk has been recognized globally for his efforts in promoting unity and compassion. He has been listed among the 500 most influential Muslims in the world and continues to inspire through his lectures and social media presence.
