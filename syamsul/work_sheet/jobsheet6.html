About

Jensen Huang is the CEO (Chief Executive Officer) of NVIDIA. As CEO, he leads the entire company and makes big decisions about what products to create and where the company should go in the future.

Jensen Huang is the founder and CEO of NVIDIA, a technology company that makes computer chips. He started NVIDIA in 1993.
